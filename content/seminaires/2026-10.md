### 13h00 - 13h50 -- Romain Yvinec (INRAe Val-de-Loire)

**A stochastic model of compartmentalized intracellular signalling**

Plasma membrane receptors are now recognized to signal both at plasma membrane and within
very dynamic intracellular organelles called endosomes. Receptors are moving from one place to
another through tightly regulated endocytosis/recycling processes. Those distinct spatial location
appear as a key regulatory mechanisms for cells to decode exracellular signals and transduce them
as physiological responses. The paradigm of ”bulk” mass-action models of signaling pathways [1]
thus need to be modified to represent highly compartmentalised systems. We have build a dynamical
model that incorporate endocytosis/recycling events within a signaling model, able to represent both
receptor trafficking in endosomes and reaction networks embedded in signaling pathways. This new
methodology is formulated as either partial differential equations (PDE) [2] or piecewise deterministic
Markov processes (PDMP), which calls for interesting new developments. In particular, as a first step
towards a better understanding of those models and their application, we have studied the long time
behavior of the model and shown the existence and uniqueness of a stationary measure under mild
conditions. We will illustrate those results through several examples.
