### 13h00 - 13h50 -- Bianca Habermann (CNRS, Univ Aix-Marseille, IBDM)

**Modeling the metabolism of bacterial warfare using constraint-based modelling**

_Myxococcus xanthus_ is a social bacterium with a very complex lifestyle that includes phases of collective or single-cell migration, kin recognition, collective predation behaviour, as well as fruiting body and spore formation during starvation. It is well known its ability to predate on other microorganisms, such as _Escherichia coli_ as a collective, a phenomenon referred to as wolf-pack predation. We wanted to understand the metabolic changes taking place in both, predator and prey, when _M. xanthus_ attacks and feeds on its prey, _E. coli_. Furthermore, we wanted to model the interaction between predator and prey using a multi-partner metabolic reconstruction in combination with constraint-based metabolic modelling. Here we will present the metabolic consequences of predation on the predator and its prey. We will present the first version of the _M. xanthus_ genome-scale metabolic model (GEM) and introduce a multi-partner metabolic network linking _M. xanthus_ to its prey, _E. coli_. Using constraint-based metabolic modelling and inspired by the Lotka-Volterra predation equations, we could recapitulate the interaction between predatory and prey and capture the population growth rates and dynamics of these two species during this predatory interaction.
